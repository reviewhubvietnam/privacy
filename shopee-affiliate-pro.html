Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Shopee Affiliate PRO

Effective Date / Ngày hiệu lực: November 13, 2026  |  Developer Contact: reviewhub.vietnam@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shopee Affiliate PRO Chrome extension ("the Extension", "we", "our") handles data when you use its affiliate link generation, content compliance checking, and media utility features.

1. Single Purpose

The Extension is designed for a single purpose: providing productivity tools for Shopee Affiliate publishers to generate shortlinks (s.shopee.vn), manage Sub_id parameters, check caption compliance, and download product media on Shopee pages.

2. Data We Process & Collect

  • Website Content (Product Links, Text, Images, Videos): When you browse shopee.vn or trigger link conversion, the Extension reads public product details (product URLs, titles, prices, images, and videos) on the active page solely to generate your affiliate shortlinks, format captions, or save media locally to your device.
  • Session Authentication: The Extension uses your existing authenticated session on affiliate.shopee.vn locally in your browser to invoke Shopee's official link-shortening API on your behalf. We never collect, read, or transmit your Shopee password, PIN, or account credentials to our servers.
  • Anonymous Hardware Device ID: For PRO license verification, the Extension computes an anonymous local hardware hash (DEV-XXXXXX) from basic system CPU/memory metadata and sends only this anonymous ID and your license key over HTTPS to our license verification endpoint.
  • Local Preferences: Your settings (caption templates, default 5 Sub_ids, compliance filters, and license state) are stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, physical address, phone number, national ID).
  • Financial, banking, or credit card information.
  • General web browsing history outside of explicit actions on supported Shopee/AI assistant tabs.
  • Keystrokes, mouse movements, or private personal communications.

4. Data Sharing & Chrome Web Store Limited Use

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade user data to any third parties.
  • We do NOT use or transfer user data for advertising, data brokering, or creditworthiness.
  • Data is transmitted only when required to perform the Extension's core user-initiated actions (calling Shopee Affiliate APIs or verifying your license status).

5. Permissions Used

  • storage: Saves user settings, templates, and license status locally.
  • tabs & scripting: Reads active Shopee product pages and injects the floating helper toolbar.
  • cookies: Uses your existing affiliate.shopee.vn session to authorize official shortlink API calls.
  • downloads: Downloads product images and MP4 videos when you click Download.
  • sidePanel, alarms, notifications: Displays the persistent side panel, schedules license checks, and notifies completion status.
  • system.cpu & system.memory: Generates an anonymous device hash for single-device license protection.
6. Contact & Data Requests:
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your license/device record, please contact: reviewhub.vietnam@gmail.com.
